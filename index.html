Frequently Asked Questions

What is the purpose of HDPE pipes in water distribution?

It is designed to transport water, gas, and other fluids with precision, allowing for leak-free distribution and long service life that is essential in municipal and industrial infrastructure projects.

What are the benefits of using HDPE pipes in manufacturing?

HDPE pipes offer exceptional chemical resistance, flexibility, and a 50+ year service life. Their fusion-welded joints eliminate leak points, making them highly cost-effective for long-term infrastructure investments.

How are HDPE pipes produced?

HDPE pipes are produced through an extrusion process using PE 100 grade resin. The raw material is melted and forced through a die to form a continuous pipe, which is then cooled, cut, and tested to meet IS 4984 and ISO 4427 standards.

What are the common applications of HDPE piping?

HDPE pipes are widely used in potable water supply, sewage systems, gas distribution, agricultural irrigation, industrial fluid transport, mining slurry lines, and telecommunications conduit installations.

Can HDPE pipes be customized for specific projects?

Yes. Mangalam HDPE pipes can be manufactured in custom diameters (20mm–1200mm), pressure ratings (PN4–PN20), SDR classes, and coil or straight lengths to match exact project specifications and standards.
